# Task T01: Multi-Hop Reasoning Chain

**Category:** Complex reasoning / Cross-domain synthesis
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Expected tokens:** ~8,000–15,000 input + ~2,000–4,000 output

## Problem Statement

Synthesize a coherent multi-hop answer to the following question by tracing through at least 3 independent knowledge domains:

> "How did the reduction in Chinese silk production during the 14th-century plague outbreaks affect Renaissance art, early modern全球经济, and the development of scientific instrumentation?"

## Requirements

1. Identify at least 3 factual causal links connecting the prompt's elements
2. For each link, cite a specific historical mechanism (not just correlation)
3. Explain the specific chain: cause → transmission mechanism → consequence
4. Flag any claims that are well-supported vs. speculative, and explain WHY
5. Identify the single greatest historiographic controversy in your chain and explain both sides

## Quality Bar

- At least 5 distinct causal chains (each with source + mechanism + consequence)
- Specificity: no vague claims ("it influenced art" — must name specific artists, works, techniques)
- Methodological humility: explicitly acknowledges what is contested vs. settled
- Total answer: 400–800 words
