# Task T04: Archival Knowledge Retrieval Under Degradation

**Category:** Memory + Retrieval / Noisy context
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Expected tokens:** ~5,000–10,000 input + ~2,000–3,500 output

## Problem Statement

> "A researcher has 3 partial notes from a degraded notebook about a critical experiment. Reconstruct what the experiment was, what the likely hypothesis was, and what the probable conclusions were. Then identify the top 3 most likely alternative interpretations of the data."

## Partial Notes (degraded source):

**Page 1 fragment:** "...subjects showed 23% higher retention at 48h when retrieval was spaced at 72h intervals vs. massed practice (p<0.01, n=87)..."

**Page 2 fragment:** "...however, the beneficial effect reversed for emotionally valenced material — particularly negativevalence stimuli — suggesting interference from arousal-mediated consolidation..."

**Page 3 fragment:** "...control group received identical stimuli at 24h intervals, equivalent to standard university exam schedules..."

## Requirements

1. Reconstruct: What experiment? What hypothesis? What were the probable conclusions?
2. Identify the 3 most likely alternative interpretations of the data
3. For each alternative: name the specific cognitive mechanism that would explain it
4. For each alternative: state what additional measurement would confirm or disconfirm it
5. Rate the confidence of the original reconstruction (and explain why the rating is what it is)

## Quality Bar

- Reconstruction must be consistent with ALL three fragments (no contradictions)
- Alternatives must be mechanistically distinct from each other
- Specificity: cite named cognitive mechanisms (not just "memory interference")
- 350–600 words
